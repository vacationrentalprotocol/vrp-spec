On 2026-11-13: V1 re-read and run at 7.1.0 (7199356), where the two discovery-document gaps are closed since 7.0.14 (2f29ba7); the three nodes measured again; and a new section, «Requirements 1.0 adds or changes», with a run of every verifier over the fifteen offer vectors of the candidate. One row per normative MUST in spec/v0.1.md (55 lines carrying MUST at e815600) and spec/well-known-uri-v0.1.md (3), and one entry per requirement that spec/v1.0.md adds or changes. It is the evidence base for the 1.0 decision record (ADR 0013, revised).

Two different things, two different words. ADR 0013 D5 marks exactly two parts at risk: §3.1 rotation and §5.3 rules.refund_schedule. At risk means the maintainers may remove the part before acceptance. A rule with fewer than two independent implementations is not at risk for that reason: this report says how many organizations implement it, and where no shipped implementation exists it says evidence outstanding. Several products of one organization count once. This report is data; it decides nothing.

How to read it. ✔ the implementation does what the rule says, with the evidence in the notes (a live measurement with UTC, or a file and line at a commit); ✗ it does not; – the rule does not address that implementation's role; ? not checked. Every claim below is one of those four.

Summary for the 1.0 record

  • Node side: every MUST has two independent products with ✔, except §3.1:165 (rotation never exercised; C not read) and §5.3:448 (one product publishes a schedule). Both are marked at risk in ADR 0013 D5, and they are the only two parts so marked. The node requirements 1.0 adds or changes (§2 version, §2.1 (a) and (b)) are met by all three nodes, two organizations.
  • Verifier side: the reference verifier (V1) and the independent verifier (V5) together cover: §1, §3.1 kid, §5.1 reference id and unknown parameters, §5.1 stale, §6 valid_until, §8, Well-Known §3. Every other verifier rule has one organization only (V1), because V5 implements the signature, the key, the payload match and valid_until and nothing else. ADR 0013 does not mark those rules at risk: they stay in 1.0, with one organization behind them until a second implements them. The gap is one implementation away: V5 adding §2 (host-owned domain, fetched domain), §5.1 (booking URL), §6 (generated_at), §7 and §9.
  • Verifier requirements 1.0 adds: evidence outstanding. No shipped verifier implements any of the three (a discovery document declaring "1.0"; the name Ed25519; an unknown or absent version in the signed offer).
