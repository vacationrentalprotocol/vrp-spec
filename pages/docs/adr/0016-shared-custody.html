ADR 0016: Shared Custody of the Standard's Handles

Status: Proposed Date: 2026-11-04 (proposed)

Related: GOVERNANCE.md §2, §3, §7, §12, MAINTAINERS.md, ADR 0002, ADR 0003, ADR 0014, ADR 0015.

This record is itself a substantive change (GOVERNANCE.md §12). It is in force only after at least seven days of review with no maintainer objecting and approval from two maintainers on its current head, and after it is merged. The hand steps in D1–D4 are taken after that, and each is measured.

Context

The standard is held by a set of handles outside this repository: the npm package agents install to verify offers, the GitHub organization, the domain, and the hosting project that serves the site. Measured 2026-11-04:

  • @vacationrentalprotocol/vrp-verify on npm has one owner, the account hemmabo (npm owner ls, 09:13:50 UTC). Versions 0.1.1 to 0.2.0 were published by the repository's workflow with OIDC trusted publishing and a provenance attestation (.github/workflows/npm-publish.yml in vacationrentalprotocol/vrp-verify at e6eceaa); 0.1.0 was published by hand from that account.
  • Pull requests in vacationrentalprotocol/vrp-spec and vacationrentalprotocol/vrp-verify are merged by the account HemmaBo-se (#180 and #22, read from the repositories).
  • The site vacationrentalprotocol.com is built from this repository in a hosting project that belongs to the reference implementer's team (read from the hosting provider's API, 08:51:44 UTC).
  • ADR 0003 says the GitHub organization "begins with two owners". The owner set is not readable from where this record was written and is to be measured by an owner before D2.

ADR 0003 moved the source out of one implementer's namespace so the standard would not "read as one implementer's product path". The handles above still do. Whoever holds the only npm owner account can publish a verifier that every agent installs; whoever holds the only hosting project can change what the site says. GOVERNANCE.md §2 commits VRP to being "not owned by, branded as, or steered for any single operator". The commitment is in the text; the handles are the proof.

Decision

D1. npm. The package @vacationrentalprotocol/vrp-verify has at least two owners who are independent of each other under ADR 0014 D2, both with two-factor authentication. Viljem Dvojmoč (@viljemd-hub), the independent maintainer in MAINTAINERS.md, is added as an owner. Publishing stays as it is: only the repository's workflow publishes, through OIDC trusted publishing with provenance; no owner publishes by hand. The repository setting that disallows token publishing is turned on where npm offers it. No single organization keeps sole rights to the package.

D2. GitHub organization. The organization vacationrentalprotocol has at least two owners who are independent of each other under ADR 0014 D2. Viljem Dvojmoč (@viljemd-hub) is added as an owner. In the repositories vrp-spec and vrp-verify, a substantive change merges only with approval from two maintainers on its current head, at least one of them independent of the change's author (ADR 0014 D1), and after its review period (ADR 0015). An editorial or non-breaking change (GOVERNANCE.md §4) needs one maintainer, who may be its author. Every change needs passing checks before merge, and an approval given on an earlier head does not count. A technical control in the two repositories is to enforce this, so that ADR 0014 D3 and ADR 0015 D2 hold in the repository itself.

Until that control is in place, the maintainer who merges checks the approvals, the approvers' independence and the review period by hand before every merge. MAINTAINERS.md "Custody" (D5) records what the two repositories enforce by themselves, as measured, and when it was last measured.

D3. Domain. vacationrentalprotocol.com carries a registrar transfer lock and auto-renewal, and a second named contact who is independent of the registrant under ADR 0014 D2 is on record at the registrar. The site's hosting project is either moved to a team named for the standard, with two owners as in D2, or the reason it stays where it is is recorded in MAINTAINERS.md.

D4. The verifier follows this governance. vacationrentalprotocol/vrp-verify is the verifier the organization publishes. Its maintainers are the maintainers in MAINTAINERS.md; a change to what it accepts or rejects is reviewed under GOVERNANCE.md §4 with ADR 0014 and the 7-day period of ADR 0015 D1; a release is tagged by a maintainer and published by the workflow (D1). GOVERNANCE.md §1, which says governance "does not govern any implementation", gains the sentence: "The verifier the organization publishes, vacationrentalprotocol/vrp-verify, is governed by this document as ADR 0016 sets out; other implementations are not."

D5. Record. MAINTAINERS.md gains a section "Custody" that lists, for each handle in D1–D3, who holds it and when it was last measured, and, for D2, whether the approvals, independence and review period are enforced by a technical control or checked by hand. A change to the custody of a handle is recorded there in the same pull request that makes it, or, for a hand step outside the repository, in the next pull request.

Consequences

  • No single account, person or organization can change the verifier agents install, the organization that holds the text, or the domain that serves it. That is what GOVERNANCE.md §2 promises, made measurable.
  • The reference implementer keeps every role it has: lead maintainer, specification editor, security contact, the reference implementation and nodes. It gives up sole custody, not a role. HemmaBo retains no sole right to any handle covered by this record.
  • Shared custody is shared power. It is bounded by the same rules: publishing only through the workflow, two approvals before a substantive change merges (D2), and GOVERNANCE.md §3 (the continuity role) and §8 (adding and removing maintainers).
  • D1–D3 are hand steps by the account holders. Each is measured after it is taken (npm owner ls, the organization's people page, the registrar's record) and written into MAINTAINERS.md "Custody" (D5). This record is not complete until every row there is measured.
  • Neutrality: the rule names no company; it applies to whoever holds a handle, the reference implementer included (ADR 0002).
